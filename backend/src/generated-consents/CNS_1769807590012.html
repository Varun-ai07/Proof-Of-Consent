Overview

Prostatectomy is a procedure performed to address a specific medical need. It is explained in simple steps so you can feel informed and comfortable.

Procedure Steps

  • Step 1: Anesthesia — You will receive anesthesia to keep you comfortable and pain-free during the procedure.
  • Step 2: Small Incisions — Small surgical incisions are made to allow access for the instruments needed.
  • Step 3: Procedure — The main surgical steps are performed carefully by your experienced surgical team.
  • Step 4: Closure — Incisions are closed with dissolvable stitches or surgical glue.
  • Step 5: Recovery Start — You begin recovery with monitoring and pain management in the recovery room.

Risks

  • Infection (2-5%): Minor infection at the incision sites can occur but is treatable with antibiotics.
  • Bleeding (1-2%): Rare internal bleeding that may require monitoring or additional care.
  • Anesthesia Reaction (less than 1%): Very rare allergic or adverse reaction to anesthesia medications.

Alternatives

  • Open Surgery — Traditional surgical approach with one larger incision instead of small ones. When minimally invasive approach is not suitable for your condition
  • Medical Management — Medication-focused approach to manage the condition without surgery. Only for select cases where surgery can be deferred or avoided

Recovery

Most patients go home within 2 days and resume light activities within a week. Full recovery typically takes about 1-2 weeks depending on the specific procedure and individual healing.

Timeline

  • Day 0: Surgery day with monitoring in recovery room. Rest and pain management.
  • Day 1: Light walking encouraged. Soft diet. Pain control medications as prescribed.
  • Day 2-3: Often ready for discharge. Follow wound care instructions carefully.
  • Week 1: Most routine activities can resume. Continue following medical restrictions.

Do

  • Take all prescribed medications as directed
  • Walk lightly as advised by your medical team
  • Keep incisions clean and dry
  • Attend all follow-up appointments
  • Rest and get adequate sleep

Don't

  • Lift heavy objects (more than 10 lbs)
  • Do strenuous exercise or sports
  • Submerge incisions in water until cleared
  • Drive until approved by your doctor
  • Ignore signs of infection or complications
